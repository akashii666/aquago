One app for the whole station

Customers, riders and the station admin all use the same app. Your account decides which screens you see.

For customers

  • Browse gallons, refills, bottled water and accessories
  • Cart and checkout with your saved address and landmark
  • Live tracking with the rider's position and ETA
  • Order history, receipts and gallon returns

For riders

  • List of assigned deliveries with address and items
  • Call or text the customer, open directions in Maps
  • Mark delivered or can't deliver, then retry
  • Share live location while on the road

For the station

  • Dashboard of today's orders and deliveries
  • Inventory, gallon counts and low-stock alerts
  • Customers, riders, payments and reports
  • Changes appear on every phone in realtime

How it works

From order to doorstep in four steps.

  1. Create an account

    Sign up with your email, name, number and delivery address.

  2. Place an order

    Pick your water and how many, then check out. Pay cash when it arrives.

  3. The station prepares it

    The admin confirms your order and assigns a rider.

  4. Track your rider

    Watch the rider on the map with a live ETA until delivery.

FAQ

Does it need the internet?

Only for the map. Everything else works on any Wi-Fi, on mobile data, or with no connection at all.

Where is my data saved?

On your phone. Your orders, accounts and settings stay on the phone where you made them.

How do riders log in?

The station adds each rider in the admin side and gives them a rider-app login.

Android says the app could be harmful. Is it safe?

Android shows this for any app installed outside the Play Store. Download the APK only from this page, then choose Install anyway.

The QR code doesn't scan.

Turn up your screen brightness and hold the camera about 20 cm away. If your camera has no QR scanner, use Google Lens, or type this page's address into your phone's browser.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery. The rider's screen shows the exact amount to collect.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen. If no code arrives, contact the station and they can reset it for you.

How do I update the app?

On iPhone, the app is always up to date. On Android, the APK does not update itself: download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one. Your account stays the same.

Why can't I download the APK on my iPhone?

APK files are for Android only. On iPhone, tap Open on iPhone instead and add it to your Home Screen.
